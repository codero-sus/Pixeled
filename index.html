How to play

  • Move with WASD, look with the mouse.
  • Dig by holding left click on a block; place with right click.
  • Pick a block with the middle mouse button, or press 1–9.
  • E flips to the second page of blocks (glass, bricks, glowstone and friends).
  • Space jumps; tap it twice to fly, Shift sinks, Ctrl sprints.
  • V switches between first and third person.
  • Water is worth a swim; caves hide glowing stone.
  • Everything saves itself while you play.
