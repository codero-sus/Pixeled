Pixeled 3D

Generating a brand new world…

Every world is different: hills, caves, ores, forests, deserts and snowy peaks are all rolled fresh from a random seed.

How to play

WASDWalk around
SpaceJump (or fly up)
ShiftSprint (or fly down)
FToggle flying
Left clickMine the block you are aiming at
Right clickBuild with the selected block
1–9Choose a block, or scroll
Middle clickPick the block you are looking at
Left clickHold to break — time depends on the block
CtrlCrouch and walk slowly
ZHold to zoom in
CCrouch too, for laptops without a Ctrl key
EscRelease the mouse

On a phone or tablet: drag the screen to look, use the thumb-stick to walk, and tap the MINE, PLACE, JUMP and ✈ buttons.

Your world saves automatically — seed plus every block you changed. Press New world any time for a completely different random landscape.
